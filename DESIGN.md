# Design Direction

## Project & Purpose
KEELBREAK is an original top-down naval tactics game. Turn your frigate to align its port or starboard guns, fire a broadside, rescue drifting salvage, and defeat a command dreadnought after four escalating encounters.

## Audience & Vibe Read
Desktop arcade players. Nautical, tense, graphic, readable. Native HTML, CSS and Canvas 2D. All art and audio are generated by game code. No network or external assets at runtime.

## Visual Dials
- DESIGN_VARIANCE: 7
- MOTION_INTENSITY: 6
- VISUAL_DENSITY: 6

## Typography
- Display: Impact with condensed sans fallback, used for the title and wave name.
- Body: system sans-serif, 1.5 line height.
- Numeric HUD: Consolas / monospace.

## Color System
- Background: #081a23
- Surface: #102c37
- Text: #e1ece7
- Accent: #b6d278
- Threat semantic color: #e18a70
- Sea depth, currents, shallow sand and islands remain dark to preserve contrast.

## Composition & Canvas
Full viewport playable sea with a floating command strip and docked bottom HUD. Title overlay occupies the left third, leaving the right two thirds as an animated illustration generated by the actual game renderer. Wide desktop canvas, responsive compact HUD. UI corners use 4px radius. Menus stack below 768px.

## Motion & Accessibility
Ship wake communicates velocity. Cannon recoil, hit flashes and water ripples communicate damage. Reduced motion disables camera shake and decorative spray. Keyboard focus visible; controls available on title and pause screens. Pause on focus loss. Audio starts only after input and can be muted. Avoid flashing full-screen effects.

## Copy Rules
English game UI. Plain action verbs and exact controls. No em dashes, promotional claims or fake metrics.

## Forbidden Patterns
No SaaS card grid, stock art, external fonts, fabricated screenshots, purple glows, login gates or network dependencies. Game rules override unrelated website route conventions.
